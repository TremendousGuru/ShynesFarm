More Than Farming — Building The Future Of Nigerian Agriculture

Founded by ENGR. ADESHINA O. OGUNDEYI, SHYNE LIVESTOCK FARMS VENTURES was established with a vision to contribute to Nigeria's agricultural growth by connecting quality farm production with the needs of modern customers and businesses.

Agriculture is more than producing food. It is about creating opportunities, supporting communities, and building reliable systems that connect farmers with people who depend on quality agricultural products.

Through responsible practices, continuous improvement, and meaningful partnerships, we are working towards becoming a trusted agricultural supply partner for customers within Nigeria and beyond.
