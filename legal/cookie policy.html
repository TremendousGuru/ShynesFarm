1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how SHYNE LIVESTOCK FARMS VENTURE uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit and use our website.

We aim to be transparent about the technologies used by our website and to avoid describing technologies or tracking practices that are not actually implemented.

This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and other applicable legal information published on this website.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files or pieces of information that a website may store on a visitor's device when the visitor accesses the website.

Cookies can be used for different purposes, including:

  • helping a website function properly
  • remembering preferences
  • maintaining a session
  • understanding how visitors use a website
  • measuring website performance
  • providing personalised content
  • supporting advertising or marketing activities

Not every website uses all of these types of cookies.

The technologies used by a website may also include other forms of browser or device storage that perform functions similar to cookies.

3. Cookies Used by Our Website

At the time this policy was last updated, SHYNE LIVESTOCK FARMS VENTURE does not intentionally operate a website-wide analytics, advertising or marketing-cookie system.

The website does not currently use services such as:

  • Google Analytics
  • Meta Pixel
  • advertising tracking pixels
  • behavioural advertising cookies
  • marketing cookies
  • third-party advertising networks

We therefore do not describe these technologies as being active on the website when they are not part of the current implementation.

4. Browser Storage and Similar Technologies

Certain website functionality may use browser storage rather than traditional cookies.

For example, parts of the website may use session storage to support temporary website functionality, including product-display behaviour.

Session storage is different from a traditional cookie.

Information stored in session storage is generally associated with the current browser session and is not intended to function as an advertising or cross-site tracking mechanism.

The use of browser storage may change as the website's functionality develops.

Where a future feature introduces a materially different form of storage or tracking, this policy may be updated accordingly.

5. Essential Website Functionality

Some technologies may be necessary for the website to function correctly.

These technologies may support things such as:

  • page functionality
  • navigation
  • temporary session-related behaviour
  • user interactions
  • displaying website content correctly

Where such technology is necessary for the operation of the website, disabling it may affect some website functionality.

We do not use the existence of essential website functionality as a basis for claiming that the website operates an advertising or behavioural-tracking system.

6. Analytics and Website Measurement

At the time this policy was last updated, the website does not have an implemented Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar third-party analytics platform that we have identified as being active on the site.

This means we do not currently describe visitor activity as being collected through a dedicated website analytics platform.

If analytics functionality is introduced in the future, this policy will be reviewed and updated to explain the relevant technology and its purpose.

7. Advertising and Marketing Cookies

The website does not currently operate a dedicated advertising-cookie or behavioural-marketing system.

We do not currently use the website to place advertising cookies for the purpose of building advertising profiles based on visitors' activity.

If this changes in the future, the relevant cookie and privacy information will be updated before or when the applicable functionality is introduced, as appropriate.

8. Contact Forms and Third-Party Services

Our website includes contact and enquiry functionality that may transmit information to third-party service providers used to process those enquiries.

The current contact form uses Formspree to process submitted form information.

Formspree is separate from the SHYNE website itself.

When a visitor submits a contact or enquiry form, information entered into that form is transmitted to the relevant third-party service for processing.

This Cookie Policy does not claim that Formspree uses no cookies or other technologies on its own systems.

Third-party services operate according to their own technologies and policies.

For information about how personal information submitted through our forms is handled, please refer to our Privacy Policy and the applicable policies of the relevant third-party service.

13. Managing Cookies and Browser Storage

Most modern web browsers provide settings that allow users to manage cookies and certain forms of browser storage.

Depending on the browser, you may be able to:

  • view stored cookies
  • delete cookies
  • block cookies
  • allow cookies only from selected websites
  • clear website data
  • control certain forms of site storage

The exact controls available depend on the browser and device being used.

Disabling certain website technologies may affect the functionality of some websites.

Because SHYNE does not currently operate a dedicated advertising or analytics-cookie system, changing browser cookie settings may not affect all website functionality in the same way.

14. Cookies and Personal Information

A cookie or similar technology may sometimes be capable of being associated with information about a visitor.

Our Privacy Policy explains how SHYNE handles personal information that customers provide through our website, including information submitted through our contact and enquiry forms.

The existence of a cookie or browser-storage technology does not by itself mean that SHYNE is collecting personally identifiable information through that technology.

Where personal information is collected, it will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection requirements.

15. Children's Privacy
